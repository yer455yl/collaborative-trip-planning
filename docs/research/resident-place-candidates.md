# Resident Suggested Place Candidates

**Source:** 15-response questionnaire summary inspected on 22 October 2026.  
**Status:** candidate leads for verification, not an operational catalog or independently verified travel recommendations.

The [survey report](survey-results.md) explains the sample and limitations. The list below groups suggestions across open-text questions. It includes no participant identifiers, original timestamps or attribution of an answer to a person. No frequency/popularity ranking is claimed.

## Normalization Rules

Merge obvious spelling/transliteration variants only: Baiterek/Bәйтерек/Байтерек; Khan Shatyr/Ханшатыр/Хан Шатыр; Botanical Garden/Бот сад/Ботаникалық бақ; Tary/Тары; Bahandi/Баханди; Kausar/Каусар/Кәусар; Ailand/Айлэнд. Preserve uncertain names rather than silently resolving a business or branch. Do not assume two similarly named parks or the EXPO area/museum are one identical venue.

Suggestions are not proof that a place is open, affordable, child-friendly or suitable in bad weather. Validate the exact establishment/branch, address, coordinates, category, opening schedule, visit duration, cost unit/currency and source/check date before including a record in generation. For provider data, check usage/storage permissions before import. Do not copy a surveyed price range into every cafe's price field.

Kudafest and kokpar outside the city were also mentioned. These are event categories or leads; no specific 2026 event date, ticket price or availability has been verified. Only insert an event after checking an authoritative schedule for the selected trip dates. Out-of-city events need a scope decision.

## Exclusions and Contrary Feedback

- Kosshy and Burabay are out-of-Astana suggestions; exclude from the initial Astana-only catalog unless the team explicitly changes scope.
- Generic answers such as “everywhere,” “all,” “shopping centers,” “stay home,” “I do not know,” and unclear abbreviations do not identify a catalog record. Do not invent a place to complete them.
- Several responses identify famous sites or shopping centers as overrated; other responses reject the premise or suggest that places are underestimated. Keep recommendation explanations and editing available rather than declaring one universal must-see route.
- Staying home in poor weather is a preference, not a missing-data error. Indoor or outdoor suggestions still require season/date checks.

This file satisfies the research step of recording resident-suggested leads. It does not satisfy the later implementation task of deploying a verified places database (DIP-29).
